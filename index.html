Shiftly Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Zeecrest, the individual developer who publishes Shiftly, is responsible for how the Android app uses your personal information. Shiftly helps you record shifts, calculate earnings and prepare client invoices. This policy explains where your records go, how advertising works and how to delete your information.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email Zeecrest at zeeshanmuhammad94@gmail.com. To request deletion, go directly to the account and data deletion instructions.

Your account and work records

You must sign in to use Shiftly’s work-tracking and invoicing features. You can use your email address and password or sign in with Google. If you choose Google, Google authenticates you. Shiftly uses account information, including your email address, to identify your account and associate your records with it.

Shiftly stores your work records online using Google’s Firebase services. We use the shift dates, hours and pay rates you enter to track time and calculate earnings. We use the client and billing details you enter to prepare invoices. If you export or share an invoice or timesheet, the people and services you send it to may keep their own copies.

Google describes how it handles information and protects data in its Firebase privacy and security information.

Advertising and your choices

Shiftly is supported by Google AdMob and has no ad-free setting. When Shiftly requests an ad, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share your IP address, device or account identifiers (including the Android advertising ID when available), app and ad interactions, and diagnostic data such as crashes and loading times. Google can use your IP address to estimate your general location. These data help deliver and measure ads, understand performance and prevent fraud.

Where consent is required, Shiftly presents an advertising consent message. You can use it to decide whether your data may be used for personalised ads. If you decline personalisation, you may still see ads. Where applicable, you can revisit your choices through the app’s advertising privacy options. Withdrawing consent applies to future processing.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services and read Google’s Privacy Policy.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we use your email address, message and any attachments to respond. If you use our deletion form, Tally receives your submission on our behalf. We use the details you provide to locate your account, verify ownership and handle the request. Please leave passwords and copies of invoices out of support messages and forms.

Delete your account or selected data

In the app: Open Settings → Profile → Delete Account to request deletion of your account and its associated data.

On the web: Use the Shiftly account and data deletion form. You can request deletion of your entire account or identify specific records to remove while keeping your account. You can use the form even after uninstalling Shiftly.

We keep your work records so they remain available through your account. Once we verify that you are the account holder, we permanently delete the requested Shiftly account or records and the associated data covered by your request within 30 days of verification. Uninstalling the app alone leaves your online account in place. Copies you previously shared with other people remain under their control.

Your rights and the reasons we use your data

Where UK or EEA data protection law applies, we process account and work-record information to provide the service you request. We rely on our legitimate interests in answering support enquiries and protecting accounts when checking who submitted a request. We rely on consent for personalised advertising and access to information on your device where consent is required. We also process information when necessary to meet legal obligations, including responding to applicable privacy rights requests.

Providers may process information outside your country. For details about processing locations and safeguards relevant to your data, contact Zeecrest using the email above. Google describes its international data transfer safeguards.

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or receive a portable copy of your information, restrict its use, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Where a change requires fresh notice or consent, we will provide notice or seek consent before using information in the new way.
